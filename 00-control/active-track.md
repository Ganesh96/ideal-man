The target companies supplied by the learner are Google, Netflix, Amazon, Tesla, Microsoft, Palo Alto Networks, GEICO, and Walmart. Titles and interview loops vary. Prepare transferable skills, then add role-specific overlays from current official material and recruiter guidance.

## Next Training Action

Run a baseline diagnostic in a live session: one unseen C# coding problem; a 10-minute design prompt; a 90-second explanation of one truthful project; and one behavioral/situational prompt. Use results to select the first two weak areas.

## Teaching Method

For each topic: problem → mechanism → preconditions → alternatives/trade-offs → failure mode → worked example → learner attempt → correction → delayed retest.

Prefer deliberate retrieval and varied follow-ups to passive reading. Use templates as scaffolds, then practice without them.

## Evidence Rule

Distinguish observed work, team results, and hypothetical designs. Never turn a practice architecture, estimate, or public scale claim into personal experience.
