## Cross-report skill clusters

- DSA under time pressure: hash/frequency maps, sliding windows, graphs/DFS/DP, correctness, complexity, optimization and follow-ups.
- Existing-code work: extend a design, understand an unfamiliar codebase, diagnose and validate a fix.
- API/data integration: parsing, validation, pagination, partial errors, retries, idempotency and deduplication.
- Design depth: product-catalog ingestion, rate limiter, concurrency and workload-specific trade-offs.
- Communication: clarify scope, narrate assumptions and changes, explain dead ends and limitations.
- Behavioral depth: specific personal contribution, constraints, stakeholders, alternatives, evidence and result.

Use these as practice surfaces, not predictions. Confirm current process with the recruiter. Never infer recording/transcription or scoring policy from an AI assistant being present. Keep hypothetical designs separate from real work history.
